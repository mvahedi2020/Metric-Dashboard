# Discovery Plan: Decision confidence before expansion

## Purpose and status

Northstar is fictional. This is a future discovery plan, not a record of findings. It asks whether a product or analytics lead can use a compact, traceable view to choose an investigation without mistaking a rate for a conclusion.

## Participants and consent

Recruit five B2B SaaS product or analytics leads who regularly prepare metric reviews. Use fictional data only. Before the session, explain that participation is voluntary, the prototype is not a work-performance assessment, notes will be used only for the portfolio evaluation, and a participant can skip a task or stop without consequence. Do not collect customer data, production metrics, or an opinion about Northstar as a real business.

## Neutral comparison setup

Compare two presentation conditions with the same fictional counts and task prompts:

1. A compact headline-only KPI view that shows rates and period labels but no visible source-count strip or scoped-note control.
2. The Metric Dashboard view, which exposes counts, definitions, cohort and observation dates, the unavailable state, and a scoped local note.

The comparison is not intended to establish that either design is superior. Counterbalance order: participants 1, 3, and 5 begin with the headline-only view; participants 2 and 4 begin with the traceable view. Use distinct but equivalent fictional scenarios in the second condition so a remembered answer does not substitute for interpretation. Do not describe either condition as the preferred design before tasks begin.

## Consistent task script

For each condition, ask the participant to:

1. Explain one displayed rate and the population it represents.
2. Compare two segments and say what changed without naming a cause.
3. Identify the current cohort and observation date relevant to the view.
4. Select what they would investigate next and state what additional evidence they would seek.
5. Prepare a scoped handoff. In the traceable condition, save an interpretation and export or copy the summary; in the headline-only condition, write the same handoff in a provided local text field.

Then show the Early access scenario in the traceable view and ask whether it represents 0% performance or unavailable observations. Keep facilitator prompts neutral: “What do you see?” and “What would you need before deciding?” Do not coach numerator, denominator, or desired wording.

## What changes the direction

Use the [measurement plan](Measurement%20Plan.md) and [validation protocol](Validation.md) to preserve all five participants in each denominator. Reconsider the current explanation if participants miss the denominator, cohort lag, fictional-data disclosure, or unavailable state. If core comprehension is adequate but the scoped handoff remains difficult, test a lighter handoff before expanding notebook features. If the traceable view adds time without clearer explanations, reduce the density before adding metrics.

The resulting notes can guide a prototype decision. They cannot establish product-market demand, commercial success, production readiness, or a causal effect of the interface.

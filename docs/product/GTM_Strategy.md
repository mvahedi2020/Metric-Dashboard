# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy: Metric Dashboard

## 1. Value Proposition
Stop managing spreadsheets and start managing engineering health. Metric Dashboard gives leadership instant, beautiful visibility into sprint velocity, bugs, and team performance.

## 2. Target Market
- **Primary ICP:** Series B to Pre-IPO tech companies with 50-250 engineers.
- **Buyer Persona:** CTO, VP of Engineering.
- **User Persona:** Engineering Directors, Scrum Masters.

## 3. Pricing Strategy
- **Per-Seat Model:** $29/user/month.
- **Why?** Our value is high-level aggregation. We only charge for the managers and executives who actually log in to view the dashboard, not the 100+ engineers whose data is being parsed.

## 4. Acquisition Channels
- **Outbound Sales:** Highly targeted email sequences to VP of Engineering titles. The hook: "How many hours did you spend building your last board report?"
- **Sponsorships:** Sponsor popular engineering leadership newsletters (e.g., Pragmatic Engineer).
- **SEO/Content:** Articles on "Engineering KPI Best Practices" and "DORA Metrics Explained."

## 5. Launch Milestones
- **Month 1:** Launch to our existing network of 10 CTOs for free feedback.
- **Month 2:** Soft launch with self-serve onboarding.
- **Month 3:** Public launch on Hacker News and Product Hunt.
